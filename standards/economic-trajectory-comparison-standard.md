# Economic Trajectory and Comparison Standard

## Purpose

This standard governs independent national economic trajectory records and later cross-country comparison.

## Required order

```text
country observation
-> country-specific mechanism
-> affected-population effect
-> controls and contrary evidence
-> reviewed national finding
-> definition reconciliation
-> comparison hypothesis or finding
```

A comparison may discover an evidence gap, but it may not fill that gap by borrowing the other country's outcome.

## Measurement discipline

Every quantitative observation must identify:

- indicator and concept;
- reported value and unit;
- nominal, real, PPP, chained, index, or not-applicable basis;
- total, per-capita, per-household, median, percentile, sector, or other denominator;
- geography and population scope;
- observation interval and release date;
- source receipt and revision posture.

Qualitative observations must identify the exact source language or bounded paraphrase and may not be silently converted to a numeric value.

## National findings

A national finding requires:

- one or more admitted observations;
- a country-specific mechanism with evidence;
- affected populations and direction of effect;
- controls and alternative explanations;
- contrary or disconfirming evidence;
- bounded confidence;
- review state.

Aggregate strength and household security must remain separate dimensions.

## Comparisons

A candidate or reviewed comparison must reference reviewed national finding identifiers from both countries. Raw observation identifiers are prohibited as comparison endpoints.

Comparison must reconcile:

- definition;
- currency and price basis;
- denominator;
- population coverage;
- time interval;
- revision vintage;
- institutional context;
- mechanism;
- distribution of effects.

## Lag claims

Lead or lag is measured per aligned indicator or reviewed finding. A single global lag may be promoted only after sensitivity analysis shows it remains stable across a declared indicator set and baseline choices.

## Authority

Automation may retrieve, normalize, validate, identify gaps, and propose hypotheses. It may not publish a finding, infer missing effects, or convert temporal sequence into causation.

## Automated research lanes

National automation must use an allowlisted source registry whose source jurisdiction matches the lane. Each captured revision is discovery-only until a reviewer admits its content as an observation and records definition, period, revision, limitations, and effect posture.

Automation may:

- fingerprint declared official sources;
- detect changed, stale, unavailable, or unexpected source responses;
- route a source event only to its declared national gap;
- retain a provenance receipt and create a review-required candidate.

Automation may not:

- attach a Canadian source to a U.S. national gap or the reverse;
- install an external-source adapter in the comparison overlay;
- convert a source revision into a national finding;
- close a gap, infer an effect, compare countries, or publish without governed review.
