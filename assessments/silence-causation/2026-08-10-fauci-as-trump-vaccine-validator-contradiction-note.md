Status: `research_candidate`

Canonical case: `ERL-SCA-2026-07-29-FAUCI-HSGAC`

## Purpose

Preserve a proposition-specific record showing how the Trump 2020 campaign and White House publicly used Anthony Fauci as a credibility source for vaccine safety, scientific independence, and the administration's COVID response while the current 2026 political accountability narrative places Fauci under exceptional scrutiny.

This note does not establish bad faith, concealment, criminal culpability, vaccine-injury causation, or motive. It records the historical rhetorical role assigned to Fauci by Trump's own campaign and contrasts it with the current attribution environment.

## Interaction with the 60-day pressure record

Separate ERL notes currently preserve evidence that:
- FDA's two-month criterion was a substantive safety-observation requirement;
- senior HHS/White House officials objected, including specific `60 days` pushback attributed by Hahn;
- Trump publicly attacked FDA rules because they made approval before Election Day harder;
- Slaoui later defended the 60-day requirement as appropriate.

This note adds a distinct rhetorical fact: **during and after that conflict, Trump's campaign repeatedly invoked Fauci as proof that the vaccine process was safe, scientific, and apolitical, and characterized political opponents who questioned it as anti-science / dangerous / anti-vaccine.**

That juxtaposition does not establish that campaign personnel knew the details of the 60-day dispute or that Fauci knew of all White House pressure. Those knowledge edges require separate evidence.

## Bounded conclusion

The 2020 Trump campaign did not merely tolerate Fauci as one member of a large response team. It repeatedly **used his scientific authority as affirmative evidence** that Operation Warp Speed and the vaccine-development process were safe, evidence-based, and free from political interference; it also used Fauci's statements to attack Biden/Harris vaccine skepticism as dangerous or anti-science. Fauci rejected at least one campaign use that implied a personal political endorsement.

This creates a material historical comparator for the current Fauci accountability narrative and should be preserved as a rhetoric/authority collision rather than converted into a motive or culpability conclusion.
