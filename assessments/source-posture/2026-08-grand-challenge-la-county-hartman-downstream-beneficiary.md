## Why this record exists
The USDA New World Screwworm Grand Challenge selection list identifies Los Angeles County Department of Public Health projects. County records reveal a private downstream beneficiary that is not visible in USDA's high-level selection list: Hartman Veterinary & Public Health Consultants, Inc. This file preserves the funding path and the distinction between a newly visible corporate entity and the longstanding subject-matter experience of the named expert.

## Observed funding/contract path
Los Angeles County Department of Public Health prepared to accept two USDA-APHIS Grand Challenge cooperative awards for:

1. NWS surveillance in dogs and other companion animals imported through Los Angeles International Airport (LAX); and
2. the NWS-PRISM countywide sentinel surveillance initiative.

County materials authorized acceptance of the two USDA awards at a combined estimated ceiling not to exceed $10,000,000, with final award amounts to be determined by USDA-APHIS. The same records authorized a sole-source contract with Hartman Veterinary & Public Health Consultants, Inc. with an estimated maximum obligation of $686,400 through April 30, 2028. County records state the Hartman contract would be 100% funded with USDA-APHIS funds.

Sources:
- Los Angeles County Department of Public Health Board packet, June 30, 2026: https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/clusteragendas/1209774_FinalAgendaPacket06.03.2026-HMHS.pdf
- Los Angeles County advance sole-source notification, April 21, 2026: https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/bos/bc/1206779_NOIMemo04-21-26_08679_AdvanceNOISSContractwithHartman.pdf

## Sole-source rationale preserved by the County
County materials state that Hartman would provide access to principal advisor Emily Pieracci, DVM, for investigative and surveillance work at LAX. The County characterized Hartman as the only vendor able to provide Dr. Pieracci in that capacity because no other vendor employed or retained her.

This is an expert-specific sole-source rationale. It is not equivalent to a finding that no other veterinarian or public-health consultant could perform any comparable work, and it does not establish that the County's determination was unreasonable. The correct discriminator is whether the statement of work required Dr. Pieracci-specific knowledge/continuity or could have been satisfied by functionally equivalent expertise.

## Material interpretation
This downstream trace changes the earlier beneficiary picture in one important way: not all private economic beneficiaries of the Grand Challenge are necessarily listed as direct USDA award recipients. Public/government recipients may subcontract or sole-source portions of their cooperative-agreement work to private firms.

Accordingly, a complete beneficiary audit must reconstruct:

`USDA award -> direct recipient -> subaward/contract -> private entity -> principals/owners -> deliverable -> amount`

rather than stopping at USDA's 40-project selection list.

## What is not established
- No evidence in this record establishes corruption, favoritism, political steering, fraud, waste, or an illegitimate business.
- A recent SAM registration is not proof of recent incorporation.
- A newly formed or recently registered consulting company can lawfully contract based on the expertise of its principals.
- The County's expert-specific sole-source rationale has not yet been independently tested against the statement of work or a market-capability survey.
