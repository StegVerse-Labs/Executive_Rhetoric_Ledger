# Grand Challenge Private-Recipient Beneficiary and Ownership Update

Status: `research_candidate`
As-of: 2026-08-22
Improper-influence finding authorized: false

## Material ownership change
USDA's June 2026 Grand Challenge award-selection sheet lists `AcceleRAD Technologies, Inc.` as a selected recipient for project `APP-113469`, `Enhancing Sterile New World Screwworm Fly Production Through High-Throughput Electron-Beam Irradiation and Automated Tray Handling`.

Public corporate sources show that AcceleRAD had been acquired by Reveam, Inc. on 2026-03-19, approximately three months before USDA announced the selected projects. AcceleRAD's own current website states that it has been acquired by Reveam. Reveam's acquisition announcement describes AcceleRAD as joining the Reveam platform to expand accelerator engineering/manufacturing capability and scale food-safety/phytosanitary technology.

## Why this changes the beneficiary analysis
- AcceleRAD remains a real operating technical business with a history predating the Grand Challenge.
- However, the economic beneficiary cannot be treated simply as an independent AcceleRAD shareholder base after 2026-03-19 without checking the transaction structure.
- The parent/acquirer `Reveam, Inc.` becomes relevant to beneficial-ownership, lobbying, financing, conflict, and prior-federal-funding review for `APP-113469`.
- The USDA award-selection sheet's use of the AcceleRAD operating name is not itself evidence that USDA failed to know or record the acquisition; cooperative-agreement documents and SAM/UEI representations are required to determine the legal awardee and disclosed parent relationships.

## Additional context
Reveam presents itself as a food-safety and phytosanitary technology company using electron-beam treatment. Its public materials show a commercial treatment center in McAllen, Texas and continued AcceleRAD engineering/manufacturing operations in Hayward, California. This creates direct technical relevance to agricultural pest-control/sterilization applications rather than evidence of a shell-company structure.

## Required acquisition
1. Obtain the actual cooperative agreement/notice of award for `APP-113469` and identify legal recipient name, UEI, parent/ownership representation, award amount, project start date, and subrecipient structure.
2. Determine when the Grand Challenge application was submitted relative to the 2026-03-19 acquisition and whether any change-of-ownership notification/amendment was required or filed before award.
3. Trace Reveam beneficial ownership, material investors/lenders, lobbying, federal assistance/contracts, and USDA/APHIS relationships.
4. Determine whether USDA's technical and responsibility review evaluated the combined Reveam/AcceleRAD resources or AcceleRAD as a standalone applicant.
5. Preserve the acquisition as a beneficiary-routing fact only; it does not establish favoritism, concealment, or an award defect.
