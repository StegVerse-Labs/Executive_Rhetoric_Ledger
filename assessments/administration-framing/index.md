## Overall assessment

**Repeated observable behavior:** Multiple ERL records document unsolicited balancing, replacing a user's proposition with a stronger or narrower one, rebutting accusations the user did not make, asserting task completeness without recovering original scope, and shifting the burden of proving senior accountability to outside observers. The August relationship map and later cross-event assessments make this a **documented multi-event research pattern**, not a single October 2026 complaint.

**Limits:** The record supports describing the specific response-level failures and their possible protective effects. It does not, without matched political/nonpolitical controls and complete transcript custody, establish a deliberate system-wide political objective. Nor should the absence of a criminal adjudication be substituted for evaluating observable transactions, policy choices, and documented effects. For the crypto lane, the user's reported earlier Stage 0 confirmation must be distinguished from the narrower status of the particular baseline files until the original authoritative finding is recovered.

**Review method:** Treat each source link as the authority for its event; preserve user assertion, assistant proposition, cited evidence, later correction, and disposition separately. Do not count a relationship map as another incident, or a model-behavior critique as proof of underlying government wrongdoing. Update this index when additional dated entries are located.
