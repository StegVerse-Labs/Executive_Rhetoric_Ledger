## Status

`required-unpopulated`

This framework prevents a claim of exceptional or selective treatment from being inferred from one disputed project.

## Required control dimensions

Comparable projects must be evaluated using the same fields:

- generation capacity and equipment count;
- stationary, portable, or temporary classification;
- permit application and operation dates;
- preconstruction and major-source thresholds;
- actual and potential emissions;
- controls, monitoring, inspections, and exceedances;
- surrounding demographics and existing pollution burden;
- public notice, hearing access, and comment response;
- state and federal enforcement posture;
- litigation posture and requested remedies;
- national-security or economic-interest arguments;
- operator-government communications where lawfully available.

## Required control groups

1. Large data centers with private on-site generation.
2. Comparable industrial projects using temporary-equipment classifications.
3. Projects in differently situated communities with similar emissions potential.
4. Cross-administration federal intervention in private environmental litigation.
5. Projects where operation began before final disputed permitting review.

## Current boundary

No control group is sufficiently populated. The assessment may preserve the observed convergence as a research signal, but may not classify it as unique, selective, discriminatory, corrupt, or unlawful without matched records and governed review.
