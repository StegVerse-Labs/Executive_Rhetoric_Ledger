## Proposition-relative initial research

1. **Visit occurrence and leader statements:** Both official U.S. post-visit recap and independently issued PRC MFA talks account report September 24 White House talks. The PRC English MFA account records Xi's position that AI should remain subject to human control and that the two countries could cooperate despite competing capabilities. This is a claim about what an official ministry publicly attributed to the leader, not a measure of implemented AI control.
2. **Bilateral incident mechanism:** The September 25 U.S. fact sheet says the countries established an SI Dialogue and agreed to create a communication channel for SI incidents; it specifies a subsequent exchange by November 2026. A separately located, primary original-Chinese [PRC MFA September 26 eight-point summary](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/zyxw/202609/t20260926_12031616.shtml) also states agreement to establish AI dialogue and an AI-incident communication channel. The two officials' public claims agree at this level, but independently inspectable operational procedures, exchanged notices and real incident readback are not present in these six captured records.
3. **Semiconductors and dinner:** BIS's January 2026 case-by-case licensing rule predates the September talks. The White House dinner notice establishes intended business/government attendance categories, not verified participation by any named individual. A dated legislative roll-call, any precise post-summit export measure and chip shipment/revenue denominators remain separate source targets.
