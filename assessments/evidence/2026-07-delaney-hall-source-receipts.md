## Scope

These receipts support event identification and identify conflicting claims. A receipt proves that a source reported or asserted a fact; it does not automatically prove the underlying claim true.

## Receipt SR-DH-005 — DOJ use-of-force policy

```yaml
source_type: "official-policy"
publisher: "United States Department of Justice"
title: "1-16.000 - Department of Justice Policy On Use Of Force"
url: "https://www.justice.gov/jm/1-16000-department-justice-policy-use-force"
posture: "primary policy source; agency applicability must be separately established"
```

Relevant policy propositions:

- Officers may use only objectively reasonable force to gain control while protecting safety.
- Force should be used only where no reasonably effective, safe, and feasible alternative appears to exist.
- Assessment is particularized to severity, immediate threat, active resistance, and attempted flight.
- Feasible de-escalation should precede force where it would not increase danger.
- Officers have duties to intervene against excessive force and request or render medical aid.

Boundary: DOJ policy is not automatically proof of the exact policy governing every DHS or ICE employee in the video. The responsible component's own current policy must be collected.

## Source posture conclusion

The receipts establish that the recording is highly likely connected to the May 26, 2026 Delaney Hall protests in Newark and that the protest arose within a documented hunger-strike and detention-conditions dispute. They also establish reports of obstruction and thrown objects by some participants. They do not support converting those acts into blanket justification for all force or into a presumption that the underlying detention and transfer operations possessed constitutional standing.
