## Review determination

The Political Influence Tree is accepted for catalogue, evidence acquisition, and governed comparison. It is not accepted as a final factual, legal, environmental-health, civil-rights, or causation finding.

The record properly separates:

- the existence of the reported project and litigation;
- the NAACP and counsel's allegations;
- the reported state temporary-equipment posture;
- the reported DOJ national-security and economic framing;
- unresolved Clean Air Act applicability;
- environmental-justice distributional concerns;
- disparate-impact inquiry;
- intentional-discrimination proof;
- project-specific health causation;
- formal civil-rights liability.

## Accepted supportable conclusions

At this review stage, the record may state only that:

1. public sources identify a dispute over gas-turbine operations associated with xAI's Colossus 2 project;
2. the NAACP and environmental counsel allege that required Clean Air Act permits and controls were absent for some operations;
3. public reporting describes Mississippi's temporary or portable equipment position;
4. public reporting states that DOJ sought dismissal and invoked economic, military, national-security, energy-independence, or innovation interests;
5. the affected-community and respiratory-burden claims warrant reproducible environmental-justice review;
6. the combination of private infrastructure objectives and governmental justification is an admissible subject for executive-rhetoric and institutional-alignment analysis.

## Prohibited promotions

The record must not yet state as established fact that:

- xAI violated the Clean Air Act;
- the turbines legally constituted one aggregated major stationary source;
- state or federal officials intentionally discriminated against Black residents;
- DOJ intervention was unlawful or corrupt;
- turbine emissions caused identified illnesses or deaths;
- the project received treatment materially different from valid controls;
- national-security language was pretextual;
- Elon Musk personally directed any state or federal action.

## Evidence defects and open requirements

Critical gaps remain:

- signed MDEQ permits, applications, schedules, technical support documents, and correspondence;
- a turbine-by-turbine inventory and operating history;
- EPA applicability, aggregation, enforcement, and Region 4 records;
- the complete federal docket, including the DOJ filing and all declarations and exhibits;
- measured emissions, fuel consumption, operating hours, controls, inspections, and exceedance records;
- reproducible Census, CDC, state-health, and airshed data;
- the complete public-notice and participation record;
- Title VI or other civil-rights complaint and disposition records;
- original xAI and MZX Tech statements and technical submissions;
- comparable-project controls.

## Source-posture determination

The installed AP receipt is admissible for the reported court and institutional posture. It is not a substitute for the DOJ filing or a judicial order.

The installed NAACP and Earthjustice receipts are admissible for claim text, asserted injury, litigation theory, requested relief, and counsel's chronology. They are interested-party and advocacy sources and are not independently admissible as proof of permit violations, emissions, health causation, or liability.

## Rhetorical classification boundary

The following formulation is preserved as interpretive framing:

> The community bears the risk. The corporation receives the presumption. The government supplies the justification.

It may be used as a clearly labeled rhetorical synthesis. It must not be represented as a judicial, agency, or independently verified factual finding.

## Promotion gates

Promotion from catalogue review to a reviewed assessment requires:

1. primary permit and docket records;
2. claim-level source receipts;
3. contradiction mapping for xAI, MDEQ, EPA, DOJ, and plaintiffs;
4. reproducible emissions and demographic analysis;
5. at least one documented control cohort;
6. independent reviewer sign-off;
7. schema and cross-record validation in CI;
8. append-only correction and supersession posture.
