## Decision

The control-comparison framework is accepted as the governing method for selecting and evaluating comparator projects.

It is sufficiently developed to:

- prevent politically convenient comparator selection;
- require technical and chronological comparability;
- separate community demographics from permit and enforcement posture;
- preserve national-security intervention as an independently testable variable;
- distinguish close controls from doctrinal or near controls;
- define the minimum control population required before disparate-treatment review.

It is not evidence that any comparator actually received different treatment.

## Required conditions before activation

1. Populate at least three controls scoring 2 or 3 for comparability.
2. Include at least one predominantly white or lower-burden community control.
3. Include at least one prior-administration or different-jurisdiction control.
4. Install signed permits, technical support records, equipment scope, and operating chronology for every close control.
5. Install public-participation and enforcement records rather than relying on project summaries.
6. Record negative search results and excluded candidates to prevent silent cherry-picking.
7. Apply the same scoring rules to Colossus 2 and every control.
8. Obtain independent review of any proposed treatment-difference score.

## Blocked conclusions

Until those conditions are met, the ledger must not state that:

- Colossus 2 received preferential treatment;
- race or community demographics caused different regulatory treatment;
- the temporary-equipment theory was unprecedented;
- DOJ intervention was uniquely favorable;
- another administration would have acted differently;
- the project is a valid control for broader claims about all data centers or all AI infrastructure.

## Accepted near-term use

The framework may be used immediately for:

- source acquisition;
- comparator nomination;
- exclusion documentation;
- primary-record checklists;
- reproducible scoring preparation;
- contradiction discovery.

## Review boundary

A complete method is not a completed comparison. The framework has standing as a research and governance instrument only. Outcome activation remains blocked until controls are populated and independently reviewed.
