## Evidence posture

The matrix intentionally preserves supporting, contrary, and unresolved evidence. Money or organizational overlap is not treated as coordination by itself. Paid lobbying and explicit campaign activity establish intent to influence the named policy outcome, while any broader purpose requires separate evidence.
