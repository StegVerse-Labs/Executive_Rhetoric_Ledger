## Evidence posture

The matrix intentionally preserves supporting, contrary, and unresolved evidence. This pass materially narrows a prior cross-front claim: SVCF-to-Food & Water Watch is primary-verified at $6.5M for 2021, while an SVCF-to-Fairplay edge is not currently corroborated by Fairplay's FY24/FY25 public donor lists and therefore remains unresolved rather than established. Money or organizational overlap is not treated as coordination by itself. Paid lobbying and explicit campaign activity establish intent to influence the named policy outcome, while any broader purpose requires separate evidence.
