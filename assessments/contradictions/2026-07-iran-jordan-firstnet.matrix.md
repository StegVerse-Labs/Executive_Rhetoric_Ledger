Status: research candidate; no final finding.

## Governing rule

A contradiction is recorded only when two claims cannot both be true under the same defined terms, time window, and object identity. Differences in source precision, publication time, target description, or rhetorical framing are not automatically contradictions.

## Unresolved contradiction tests

1. **Launch-count test:** obtain launch telemetry or an authoritative Iranian numerical declaration.
2. **Target-identity test:** determine whether the two Iranian target descriptions referred to one installation, two installations, or rhetorical duplication.
3. **Interception test:** reconcile Jordanian, U.S., and Iranian track records missile by missile.
4. **Authorization test:** separate contingency-plan maturity, option selection, presidential authorization, and execution order.
5. **Telecom-common-cause test:** compare incident windows, serving infrastructure, control-plane events, backhaul failures, and federal reporting.
6. **Publication-order test:** preserve original post metadata and distinguish drafting, issuance, indexing, translation, and republication.

## Review boundary

The matrix may support a finding only after each relied-upon row has:

- at least one preserved primary-source receipt;
- source hash and custody metadata;
- normalized timestamps at the original precision;
- an explicit compatible/contradictory determination;
- and independent reviewer concurrence or documented dissent.
