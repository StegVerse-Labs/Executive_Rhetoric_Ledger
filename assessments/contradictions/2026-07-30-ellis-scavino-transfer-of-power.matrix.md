## Corroboration classes

### Direct corroboration of the conversation

None presently preserved beyond Ellis's own account. Required objects include contemporaneous messages or notes, an independent witness, a recording, or a sworn Scavino response.

### Corroboration of Scavino's access and knowledge capacity

Strong. Official congressional records establish proximity, communications responsibility, and involvement in post-election messaging. This supports capacity to know but does not prove the conversation or the accuracy of the characterization.

### Corroboration of Trump's general state of mind

Moderate to strong contextually, subject to claim-level primary sourcing. Public statements and insider accounts show persistent resistance to acknowledging defeat. This does not establish a private instruction transmitted through Scavino.

### Corroboration of a specific operational plan

Low from this chain. Any pressure campaign, certification-delay effort, alternate-elector activity, or other operational theory must be independently sourced and explicitly connected.

### Contradiction or impeachment evidence

Elapsed time, alcohol, missing contemporaneous records, cooperation incentives, Ellis's prior false-statement plea, excerpt incompleteness, and the eventual transfer of power all limit inference. None alone disproves the conversation.

### Unresolved absence

The complete proffer, plea instruments, event and access records, Ellis–Scavino communications, Trump–Scavino communications, sworn Scavino account, and claim-level January 6 exhibits remain unresolved.

## Promotion result

The matrix supports continued research and structured review preparation. It does not support publication as a finding that Trump personally ordered an unlawful refusal to leave office.
