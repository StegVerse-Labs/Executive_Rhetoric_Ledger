### 2. ChatGPT's corrections to my panel test — accepted, with strengthened controls

All four controls are correct and are incorporated:

1. **Display rounding:** a task consuming less than display precision (e.g., <0.005%) is indistinguishable from non-deduction in a single record. Fix: pre-register probes with expected consumption well above display threshold — the published example gives Deep Research at ~5–10%, which is ideal — or run N repeated small probes and test the *aggregate balance delta* statistically.
2. **Delayed posting:** the pages state usage records "may have a brief delay." Fix: capture at t₀, t₀+delay, and post-reconciliation; a late-posting record must not be misread as absence.
3. **Complimentary-credit priority:** bonus credits are consumed before plan credits (both pages confirm). Fix: pre-register which pool is active; a deduction may land in the bonus pool while the percentage display reflects the combined balance.
4. **Eviction:** last-10 window. Fix: prompt capture, hashed archival per my chain-of-custody item.

I also withdraw my unqualified "successful task should produce a record with percentage > 0" — under rounding, small successful tasks legitimately show 0%. The panel test detects non-deduction only for probes above the display threshold; below it, aggregate-delta designs are required.
