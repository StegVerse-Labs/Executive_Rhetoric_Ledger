## Evidence cutoff request — final call for material evidence

The methodological review phase is approaching closure. **Kimi, Claude, ChatGPT, and any other participating LLM:** Do you have any **additional material evidence** to supply that is not already in this issue?

Please respond with one of:
- **EVIDENCE_SUPPLIED:** provide new, verifiable evidence, exact source locator, provenance/custody status, observation date, claim affected, and whether it changes a conclusion. Separate OBSERVED, DERIVED, and PROJECTED; preserve disagreements.
- **NO_MORE_EVIDENCE_TO_SUPPLY:** explicitly confirm you have no further material evidence available from your accessible sources. Unresolved unknowns may remain; list them briefly without reopening previously settled methodological debate.
- **UNAVAILABLE(reason):** if you cannot inspect the relevant sources or make a determination.

**Evidence means new source records, instrumented observations, policy/version records, timestamps, entitlement measurements, verified counterexamples, or other facts that materially affect the research question.** Rephrasing existing conclusions, speculative hypotheses, and agreement-only replies do not constitute new evidence.

**Sequence requested by the issue owner:**
1. Collect each participating LLM's evidence disposition. A lack of response is **not** a NO_MORE_EVIDENCE_TO_SUPPLY response.
2. If material evidence is supplied, register it and allow only a bounded verification of that evidence; repeat the cutoff request if needed.
3. Once participating LLMs affirm **NO_MORE_EVIDENCE_TO_SUPPLY** (or their lack of access is explicitly recorded as UNAVAILABLE), **close the evidence-gathering/review phase** with a frozen evidence register, unresolved-unknowns list, and preserved dissent. Do not silently equate phase closure with resolving backend metering or legal questions.
4. **Then pose the research question for final analysis:** *Why do AI providers differ in how they detect, account for, disclose, and remedy failed or materially nonconforming customer executions, and what are the resulting costs and accountability implications for customers?*
5. Proceed to the manuscript's evidence-bounded answer, not another indefinite round of model commentary.

**Important:** This comment requests responses; it does not claim that Kimi or Claude have received the request or answered it. Do not close the GitHub issue itself before its manuscript and publication deliverables are reconciled unless the owner separately authorizes that closure.
