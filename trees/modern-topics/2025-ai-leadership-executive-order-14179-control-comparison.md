# Control Comparison Expansion: EO 14179 AI Leadership Framing

## Purpose

This file expands the control-comparison branch for the modern-topic Political Influence Tree:

```text
trees/modern-topics/2025-ai-leadership-executive-order-14179.md
```

The purpose is not to decide whether EO 14179 is good policy.

The purpose is to define what evidence would be required before claims about AI leadership, innovation barriers, safety policy, competitiveness, or national security can be treated as admissible comparative justification.

## Core Rule

```text
EO 14179 is admissible as an action record.
Its factual justification requires controls before comparative use.
```

## Comparison Question

```yaml
comparison_question:
  primary_question: "Did prior federal AI policy materially obstruct American AI leadership, innovation, competitiveness, or national security in a way that EO 14179 corrected or was designed to correct?"
```

## Minimum Evidence Needed for Upgraded Classification

The EO 14179 tree may move from:

```text
admissible-for-action-record; pending-controls-for-factual-justification
```

to:

```text
admissible-with-controls
```

only if the ledger receives:

- primary records identifying which prior AI actions were barriers;
- evidence that those actions had measurable effects;
- comparable prior-administration or similar-instrument controls;
- outcome evidence after EO 14179 implementation;
- source receipts showing what each source proves;
- notes distinguishing economic, safety, and national-security claims.

## Anti-Misuse Note

A missing control comparison does not prove EO 14179 is wrong.

It also does not prove prior AI policy was right.

It means the ledger may record EO 14179 as an official action and policy claim, but may not treat its factual justification as comparatively proven until controls are supplied.

## Summary

EO 14179 is already admissible as an executive action record.

This control expansion defines the evidence boundary required before the ledger may treat its claims about innovation barriers, AI leadership, competitiveness, or national security as admissible comparative justification.
