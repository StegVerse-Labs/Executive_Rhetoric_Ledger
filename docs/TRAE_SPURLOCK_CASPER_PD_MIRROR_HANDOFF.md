## Inference ceilings

- Missing public evidence is a gap, not proof of concealment or misconduct.
- Omission may be material without proving intentional deception.
- No emotional reaction, including absence of screaming, is treated as proof of motive or relationship quality.
- Possession of keys does not by itself establish or negate lawful occupancy/common authority.
- No injury does not by itself negate a possible offense, but it is relevant to severity, urgency, and exigency analysis.
- Later-discovered facts do not retroactively establish what officers knew before entry.
- A prosecutor's no-charge decision and a civil complaint are distinct evidentiary/legal objects and neither is treated as dispositive of every constitutional or factual issue.
