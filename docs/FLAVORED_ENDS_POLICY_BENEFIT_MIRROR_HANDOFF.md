## Research boundary

Do not import stronger accusations than the evidence/source actually presents.

Required distinctions:
- timing != causation;
- political benefit != regulatory causation;
- lobbying/access != quid pro quo;
- marketing authorization != safety endorsement;
- youth flavor prevalence != proof a specific authorization increased youth use;
- a changed decision != proof of which variable changed it.

The research lane is permitted to test whether these dimensions align materially and whether the same product/evidence package would resolve differently under different FDA governance states.
