## Installed rule

Every research surface must classify related domains as one of:

- `PRIMARY_INVENTORY`
- `SHARED_PRIMARY`
- `CONTROL_AND_PRIMARY`
- `CONTROL_ONLY`
- `ADJACENT_ONLY`
- `SEPARATE_SIBLING`
- `UNRESOLVED`

A domain may be a control later without ceasing to be primary evidence if the originating request included it.

A raw evidence node may belong to multiple research surfaces by immutable reference. Cross-surface use does not authorize duplicate truth objects, normalization, or overwriting.
