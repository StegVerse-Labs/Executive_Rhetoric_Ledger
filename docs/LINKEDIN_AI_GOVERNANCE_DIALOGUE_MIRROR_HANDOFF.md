## Immutable conceptual distinctions for any continuation
1. **Sovereignty is not authority.** Imposed constraints are distinct from a participant's independent capacity to exist, act, and participate.
2. **Disposition is a transition, not a permanent permission.** `DENY` is not interchangeable with `NOT ALLOW`; a correct denial may be the governed outcome.
3. **Semantic fidelity is not admissibility.** Faithful expression of human meaning and downstream state-transition eligibility are separate assessment layers.
4. **Constitutional continuity is not by definition legitimacy.** Distinguish continuity of a rule set from legitimate, governed capacity to revise it.
5. **Receipts are bounded evidence, not omniscience.** Unknown unknowns cannot be exhaustively listed or prevented by assertion.
6. **Performance, consequence-boundary behavior, semantic drift, and governance acceptance require different tests.** Never convert a local successful test into proof of deployment, interoperation, public safety, or general correctness.
7. **Support human agency.** Concern about an AI becoming a benevolent ruler or suppressing options is a separate human-factors evaluation, not solved by deterministic approval alone.
8. **Architecture pointers are references, not warranties.** StegCore, StegVerse-SDK and Governance repository links in #263 do not establish a particular CI head, integration, or runtime receipt.
9. **Fictional cases are discussion devices, not accepted unpaid collaborations, authorized joint projects, or source-verification evidence.**
