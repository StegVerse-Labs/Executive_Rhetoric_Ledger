## Purpose

## Evidence boundary

The task must keep these measures separate:

1. U.S./CENTCOM-facilitated or protected vessel movements.
2. AIS-visible vessel transits.
3. Independently reconstructed dark/off-AIS transits.
4. Non-Iranian-linked cargo-vessel subsets.
5. Crude-tanker counts.
6. Crude-only barrels/day.
7. Crude plus refined products/condensate barrels/day.
8. Gulf exports that bypass Hormuz by pipeline, Red Sea port, Oman transfer, or other route.
9. Downstream delivered/imported barrels at destination markets.
10. Pre-conflict baselines, with exact provider and date window.

A difference among these measurements is not, by itself, evidence of deception or motive.
