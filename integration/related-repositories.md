# Related Repository Network

## Purpose

`Executive_Rhetoric_Ledger` is the governed comparative compendium. The repositories below are explicitly related research, producer, oversight, historical, social-discovery, biographical, educational, or publication surfaces.

A relationship does not grant automatic evidentiary standing. Every imported item remains subject to Source Posture, Political Influence Tree, control-comparison, dispute, review, supersession, and privacy requirements.

## Boundary rules

1. A related repository may nominate evidence, incidents, controls, contradictions, adjacency links, or outcomes; it cannot accept its own material into the ledger.
2. Repository identity is provenance, not proof.
3. Social-media material may prove circulation or claim existence without proving the underlying event.
4. Scholarly interpretation does not replace primary records for event-specific findings.
5. Association and adjacency do not establish causation, influence, participation, or culpability.
6. Allegations, charges, convictions, civil findings, testimony, and institutional findings remain separate.
7. Genealogical and biographical links require privacy review and do not establish political alignment or responsibility.
8. Educational surfaces must preserve source posture, uncertainty, disputes, and supersession.
