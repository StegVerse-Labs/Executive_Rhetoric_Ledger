## Declared purpose

StegLearn is a homeschool-capable governed learning system that converts curiosity, building, observation, explanation, revision, and responsibility into parent-reviewed learning receipts.

Confirmed native loop:

```text
Wonder
-> Build or Observe
-> Explain
-> Review
-> Receipt
-> Portfolio
```

Its current repository is a design and documentation foundation with a learning-receipt schema and example receipts.

## External platform and evidence environments

Potential platform interactions include:

- learning management systems;
- digital libraries and museum archives;
- educational video and simulation platforms;
- assessment and portfolio systems;
- homeschool reporting systems;
- parent and educator review interfaces;
- AI tutors and research assistants;
- public historical and political source repositories;
- Executive Rhetoric Ledger reviewed compendium outputs.

Platform engagement, completion badges, AI answers, or test scores are not by themselves proof of learning.

## Evidence and publication boundary

StegLearn may consume reviewed ledger outputs as educational material, but must preserve:

- source posture;
- uncertainty;
- distinction between reported claim and established fact;
- age-appropriate framing;
- correction and update paths;
- parent or educator review.

StegLearn-generated learner work may provide evidence of learning, but should not become evidence for political claims in Executive Rhetoric Ledger.

## Smallest compatible boundary

```text
accepted-with-limitations or accepted ledger topic
-> educational transformation with source links and limitations
-> StegLearn lesson, activity, or learner prompt
-> parent-reviewed learning receipt
```

Return flow should be limited to educational usage, comprehension, and correction feedback—not factual promotion of learner answers.

## Open gaps

- Mirror handoff.
- Workflow and deployment mechanisms.
- Learning-receipt validator behavior.
- AI scaffold policy implementation.
- Parent authority and learner privacy controls.
- Educational content versioning and source refresh.
- Platform APIs and portfolio export contracts.
- Correction propagation from upstream ledger topics.
- Age and jurisdiction-specific homeschool requirements.

## Prohibited integration changes

- Do not publish disputed ledger material as settled educational fact.
- Do not treat learner output as political evidence.
- Do not transfer child data, portfolios, or private receipts into the ledger.
- Do not create another educational publication system inside Executive Rhetoric Ledger.
