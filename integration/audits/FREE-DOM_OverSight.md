## Declared purpose

FREE-DOM_OverSight is a private clearinghouse for protected testimony, internal observations, third-party submissions, escalation material, and pattern analysis that is not yet publicly verifiable.

Its README establishes two critical boundaries:

1. no content is presumed factual without verification;
2. no material is published directly from this repository.

Only anonymized, non-identifying, verifiable artifacts may be promoted to the public FREE-DOM repository after defined thresholds are met.

- court and administrative dockets;

No current automated retrieval, submission API, webhook, mail processor, or scheduled monitor was confirmed in this pass.

## Native evidence and privacy boundaries

The repository should retain custody of:

- protected identities;
- unredacted testimony;
- internal observations;
- sensitive attachments;
- unresolved allegations;
- private correlation work;
- escalation material.

## Promotion transition

```text
sensitive intake
-> authenticity and authority review
-> corroboration
-> privacy and retaliation-risk review
-> redaction or anonymization
-> promotion decision
-> public FREE-DOM artifact or ledger candidate
-> receipt
```

Publication or promotion is a governed transition, not a copy operation.

## Prohibited integration changes

- Do not export raw testimony or identifying material.
- Do not let correlation or pattern analysis become a factual finding without evidence.
- Do not duplicate its private intake or redaction functions in the public ledger.
- Do not create a direct public publication path.
- Do not classify it as an automated producer until current workflows and governance files are inspected.
