## Declared purpose

The repository is a structured historical identity ledger for evidence-linked lineage reconstruction. It explicitly rejects unsourced ancestry, folklore inheritance, and unsupported royal-line claims.

Confirmed native mechanisms include:

- Canonical Identity IDs;
- deterministic file naming;
- Source Registry indexing;
- evidence grading;
- cemetery indexing;
- migration-vector analysis;
- structured lineage trees;
- transparent uncertainty handling.

Every parent-child link must have a Source_ID, every claim must be graded, and every identity must have a canonical ID.

## Native evidence model

```text
Grade A = original primary record
Grade B = transcribed primary record
Grade C = secondary published source
Grade D = family narrative or unsourced claim
```

No lineage link may rest solely on Grade D evidence.

## External platform and record environments

Relevant data platforms may include:

- census image and index services;
- civil birth, marriage, and death registries;
- cemetery and memorial databases;
- military and pension archives;
- probate and land-record systems;
- church and newspaper archives;
- genealogy platforms and GEDCOM tools;
- DNA testing and match platforms;
- historical map and migration datasets;
- court, county, state, and federal archives.

Platform trees, hints, user submissions, DNA matches, and index transcriptions are discovery aids, not automatic proof.

## Privacy and identity boundaries

Living-person records require stronger restrictions than historical records. The repository must distinguish:

```text
historical deceased person
living person
private family narrative
public record
DNA-derived relationship hypothesis
verified lineage link
```

DNA data, living-person birth dates, contact information, and private family materials must not flow into a public political ledger without explicit lawful authority and necessity.

## Smallest compatible boundary

```text
reviewed historical identity or lineage record
+ canonical identity
+ source IDs and evidence grade
+ living-person and privacy posture
-> historical or biographical context candidate
-> Executive Rhetoric Ledger review
```

The ledger should receive only context necessary to distinguish identities, relationships, chronology, or historical influence—not the complete family archive.
