## Purpose

Create bounded investigative lanes for testing whether public presidential/family communications, administration policy actions, token-supply decisions, access incentives, and cross-market geopolitical rhetoric repeatedly produced measurable market transitions from which Trump-affiliated or Melania-affiliated entities could benefit.

This record does **not** presume manipulation, insider trading, corruption, quid pro quo, illegal self-dealing, or coordinated intent. It exists to preserve a reproducible event-study and beneficiary-mapping program with explicit disconfirming tests.

## Governing analytical sequence

Do not jump from correlation to motive. Evaluate in this order:

1. Did a public statement, policy action, token action, or access incentive precede a measurable market move?
2. Was the move directionally consistent with the information content of the event?
3. Was the move abnormal relative to ordinary market volatility and contemporaneous control events?
4. Did the effect persist, reverse, or decay across 5m / 30m / 1h / close / 24h / 72h windows?
5. Which entities could economically benefit through token inventory, liquidity pools, fees, sales, valuation, related ventures, or secondary holdings?
6. Did any wallets or counterparties position before the public event?
7. Are those wallets attributable to insiders, vendors, market makers, exchanges, affiliates, unrelated traders, or unresolved actors?
8. Are policy/regulatory transitions independently explainable by public policy objectives, or do they show proposition-relative conflict patterns requiring further review?
9. What evidence would weaken or falsify each hypothesis?

## Lane A — Executive rhetoric and oil-price event study

### Research question

Do repeated Trump statements that move between negotiation/peace signals and strike/sanction/escalation signals generate statistically significant abnormal moves in Brent, WTI, energy equities, inflation expectations, the dollar, gold, Bitcoin, and broad equities?

### Candidate event families

- strike postponement / claimed negotiating progress;
- ceasefire or memorandum continuation / termination;
- bombing, surrender, blockade, or sanction threats;
- claims that Hormuz or regional oil flows are normal/open despite conflicting physical-flow evidence;
- subsequent clarifications, reversals, or re-escalations.

### Current source leads

- Reuters, 2026-08-21, oil closes higher after threat to sanction countries trading with Iran: `https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-set-second-weekly-rise-unsettled-us-iran-war-crimps-supply-2026-08-21/`
- Reuters, 2026-08-18, no talks planned / Hormuz dispute: `https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-says-no-talks-planned-with-iran-tehran-says-strait-hormuz-still-shut-2026-08-18/`
- Existing ERL candidate: `research-candidates/2026-08-12-trump-administration-oil-flow-normalization-claim.md`

### Required measurements

- event timestamp to nearest available minute/second;
- Brent and WTI returns over 5m, 30m, 1h, close, 24h, and 72h;
- abnormal returns against oil-volatility controls;
- contemporaneous EIA, OPEC/OPEC+, tanker, attack, refinery, inventory, Fed, and macro events;
- reversal elasticity when later rhetoric materially changes the prior signal;
- distinction between rhetorical signal and physically observed supply-state change.

## Lane E — Family-token rotation and aggregate extraction

### Research question

Did the launch of `$MELANIA` shortly after `$TRUMP` create a family-token rotation in which aggregate trading volume, fees, sales, or retained-token value benefited related entities even while one token's quoted market value fell?

### Competing hypotheses

- H1: destructive cannibalization inconsistent with coordinated extraction;
- H2: independent launches with no coordinated economic objective;
- H3: attention/volume maximization across two family-branded assets;
- H4: liquidity fragmentation generated fees but reduced longer-term value;
- H5: launch timing was primarily political/branding activity with financial effects incidental.

No hypothesis is preferred before event-level and wallet-level evidence is reconstructed.

## Lane H — Cross-market rhetoric sequencing

### Research question

Across oil, the dollar, gold, Bitcoin, `$TRUMP`, `$MELANIA`, and related crypto ventures, is there a repeated sequence in which discretionary rhetoric or policy transitions produce predictable market repricing and identifiable private-benefit opportunities?

### Candidate derived measures

- `executive_rhetoric_market_impact_beta` — abnormal return attributable to event window after controls;
- `rhetoric_reversal_elasticity` — proportion of prior move reversed after contradictory/reversing rhetoric;
- `affiliate_value_capture_ratio` — attributable token sales + fee flow + inventory-value change relative to aggregate market turnover;
- `preannouncement_positioning_score` — strength of evidence that wallets accumulated before public disclosure beyond bot/public-discovery baselines;
- `policy_private_interest_overlap_score` — proposition-relative mapping of policy authority, private economic interest, and contemporaneous benefit without converting overlap into motive.

These are candidate research metrics, not findings.

## Lane I — Disconfirming evidence and controls

Every lane must preserve evidence that weakens the proposed pattern.

Required controls include:

- matched geopolitical statements by prior presidents;
- matched Trump statements that did **not** move markets;
- oil moves driven by attacks, inventory, OPEC, or shipping data without Trump rhetoric;
- crypto price/volume events without Trump/Melania promotion;
- comparable celebrity/political meme-coin launches;
- token-wallet prepositioning caused by public contract discovery or automated bots;
- regulatory actions adverse to Trump-affiliated crypto interests;
- policy actions with broad industry rationale and no preferential counterparty effect;
- market moves opposite the hypothesized direction.

Zero-result discovery is `no-update` unless coverage completeness is independently established.

## Promotion gates

A market-impact finding requires reproducible event timestamps, price series, controls, and abnormal-return methodology.

A beneficiary finding requires attributable economic flows or holdings, not merely token-price appreciation.

A preannouncement-information finding requires evidence that timing is not sufficiently explained by public discovery, automated trading, or unrelated information channels.

A manipulation, corruption, quid-pro-quo, insider-trading, or motive finding requires a separate evidentiary chain establishing the applicable legal/factual elements. None is authorized by this candidate.
