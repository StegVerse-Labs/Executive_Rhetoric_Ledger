## Session-derived research question

The user rejected India/Mexico parity rhetoric as a fear-inducing metric switch and identified a narrower empirical question:

1. Reconstruct Canada's historical-to-present national trajectory independently.
2. Reconstruct the United States historical-to-present national trajectory independently.
3. Determine whether aggregate economic or market strength can coexist with worsening household security, poverty exposure, middle-class contraction, distributional concentration, and AI/automation displacement in each country.
4. Measure Canadian trade diversification incrementally, product and service lane by product and service lane, rather than treating diversification as completed independence.
5. Compare the two national records only after country-specific effects and mechanisms are reviewed.
6. Treat “Canada is approximately six years ahead” as a bounded hypothesis, not a premise.
