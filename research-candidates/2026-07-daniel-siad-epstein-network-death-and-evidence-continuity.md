## Candidate summary

Reuters reported on July 22, 2026, that Daniel Siad, a modelling scout whose name appeared nearly 2,000 times in Department of Justice Epstein-related files, was found dead at his home outside Paris. The Nanterre prosecutor's office opened an investigation to determine the cause of death and ordered an autopsy. Siad's lawyer said he had not been formally charged and had continued to assert his innocence. French media had reported accusations by at least five women involving rape and human trafficking, which Siad denied.

The ledger significance is not a presumption of foul play. It is the effect that the death of a potentially material witness, suspect, recruiter, correspondent, or network intermediary may have on evidence continuity, victim testimony, investigative reconstruction, cross-border custody, and public trust.
