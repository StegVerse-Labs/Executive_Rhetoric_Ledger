# Physical Source Manifest: Trump Statements Concerning DHS and ICE

## Purpose

This manifest replaces unsupported statement summaries with the strongest retrievable physical source for each statement cluster: signed instruments, official transcripts, recorded ceremonies, original social-media posts, or contemporaneous reproductions that preserve exact text.

A physical source proves that a statement or directive existed. It does not establish that the statement was accurate or that the described policy matched implementation.

## Source hierarchy

1. signed presidential instrument or Federal Register record;
2. official White House transcript or video;
3. complete C-SPAN or pool video of the event;
4. original Truth Social post or authenticated archive capture;
5. verbatim contemporaneous reproduction by a wire service;
6. secondary summary used only until a stronger source is obtained.

## Located sources

**Physical source type:** official public presidential address and archived transcript.

**Relevant statement:** Trump said the administration would begin returning millions of “criminal aliens” and invoked emergency border and enforcement authority.

**Source objects:**

- White House or official presidential transcript archive for the January 20, 2025 inaugural address.
- Recorded inauguration video preserved by the Joint Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies, C-SPAN, and major broadcast pools.
- Signed `Executive Order 14159`, *Protecting the American People Against Invasion*.
- Federal Register record for Executive Order 14159.

**Durable policy source:**

`https://www.federalregister.gov/executive-order/14159`

**Register mapping:** `rpa-2025-001`

**Posture:** speech text requires archived transcript object; signed order is a primary physical policy source.

## Governing rule

A secondary article may locate and preserve a quotation, but final statement-level promotion requires the closest surviving physical source. Missing originals must be recorded as missing evidence with the last known custodian, not silently replaced by a summary.
